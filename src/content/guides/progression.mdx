## What develops during a run

The run loop combines consumption, module upgrades, and larger encounters. The official press kit says boss victories contribute to further castle growth. Your immediate priorities concern the fortress you are currently driving and the choices available now.

Do not assume every temporary state is permanent. A stronger attack, a larger castle, or a resource count inside a run does not by itself establish what will be present when another attempt begins.

</section>

## What unlocks between attempts

Official materials identify modules and vehicles as between-run unlocks, and the Steam description also includes artifacts and captains among the options that widen future strategies. These systems expand what you can choose; they should not all be treated as a single permanent-stat increase.

## Unlock-based rerolls in 0.9.10

The September 12 UTC announcement adds one New Module Reroll for each six unlocked modules and one Artifact Reroll for each six unlocked artifacts. These are the exact categories named by the update.

The practical significance is that collection breadth can provide additional choice, rather than only adding another candidate item.

Do not extend the rule to captains or vehicles; the announcement names modules and artifacts. See the [0.9.10 summary](/updates/#hotfix-0-9-10) for the surrounding changes.

</section>

## Difficulty is not just a different biome name

Official notes mention Grassland difficulties 1, 2, and 3, and Dark Forest difficulties 2 and 3. They identify Meadows as Grassland Difficulty 2. Those labels help distinguish the environment from a particular challenge variant.

Do not substitute an assumed order from a genre convention. Check the current game's requirement when choosing the next objective.

If a guide says a region became easier, look at the specified difficulty. The [biome records](/database/biomes/) preserve that context.

</section>

## Wins, overtime, and achievements

A normal win and an overtime attempt should be recorded separately. The September 10 achievement announcement discussed win checks that did not trigger when a player continued into overtime. The later 0.9.9 notes report fixes for the First Win and All Biomes Won achievements.

That chronology matters: an older report of a missing achievement is not proof that the issue is still present in the current build. If your result looks wrong, record the version and the sequence that produced it rather than assuming your progress disappeared.

The same week, cumulative distance, boss-kill, and consumption achievement requirements were reduced. Those are achievement adjustments, not unlock costs or a currency conversion formula.

</section>

## Plan a useful next run

Choose the next experiment based on the problem you can identify. An additional option that fills a missing function can be useful; an option you do not understand may be harder to evaluate even if it is newly available. Keep your plan tied to current descriptions.

Before changing captain and vehicle together, understand the platform's handling. Before pursuing an artifact combination, confirm the trigger. Before pushing overtime, distinguish that endurance goal from an ordinary clear.

The [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/) covers immediate survival, while [build principles](/guides/best-build/) covers comparing a setup.

</section>
