<section className="step-card" id="step-1">

## Define what best means for this attempt

A setup intended to make a first clear comfortable is answering a different question from an overtime experiment. Decide whether you want dependable survival, easier ability use, a particular offensive behavior, or progress toward a collection goal.

Early Access balance can change: the first release week already altered several modules and captain penalties. Treat an old demonstration as a starting idea, not a permanent verdict.

</section>

<section className="step-card" id="step-2">

## Build around a function you can use reliably

Begin with the attack or action that actually carries your run. Consider how often it is available, the position needed to use it, and what happens if it fails to finish a target. A useful build is more than the sum of attractive tooltip lines.

Do not confuse a module's automatic attack with its active ability. [Side Ballista](/database/modules/side-ballista/) had those two parts changed in opposite directions in 0.9.9. [Side Arm](/database/modules/side-arm/) received improvements to both in 0.9.10.

If you seldom use an active ability, first learn whether it solves an important problem before spending a run building around it. If you use it often, check how your captain affects its timing.

</section>

<section className="step-card" id="step-3">

## Recommended for projectile pressure: offense plus control

One approach worth evaluating is combining an offensive plan you already understand with a way to respond to incoming projectiles. [Force Mage](/database/modules/force-mage/) provides the cancellation behavior; the rest of the equipment should still be selected for the actual threats and options in your run.

The reasoning is defensive: an additional response may make it easier to preserve a good position. Use it when incoming shots are the problem you need to solve.

Consider this approach when failed positioning against projectiles repeatedly ends otherwise promising attempts. Reject it as a supposed solution to unrelated problems unless the current game behavior supports that use.

</section>

<section className="step-card" id="step-4">

## For ability-focused players: make openings count

An ability-focused experiment asks whether you can consistently create and use openings. [Front Barracks](/database/modules/front-barracks/) and Side Ballista have active-ability changes that make their current versions worth reading carefully.

Captain synergy is a separate check. [Huntress](/database/captains/huntress/) has an ability-cooldown drawback. Read both descriptions before claiming a combination is efficient.

For this style, evaluate the time between useful actions and the safety of the vehicle during that interval.

</section>

<section className="step-card" id="step-5">

## For confident drivers: contact-oriented play

A [Ram](/database/modules/ram/) experiment makes the vehicle's approach and exit especially important. The module's consistency fixes are a reason to use current footage and current behavior rather than assuming every older collision result still applies.

Offense, defense, and mobility are closely connected in this approach. Reaching a target is not enough if the resulting position is difficult to escape. Test the platform's response before adding a captain that changes Nitro regeneration or damage taken while boosting.

This is not a recommendation to pair Ram with any specific captain. In particular, [Duelist's](/database/captains/duelist/) boost-related drawback is a fact to account for, not proof of a favorable interaction.

</section>

<section className="step-card" id="step-6">

## Range, area pressure, and boss-focused decisions

Do not label an attack best for bosses simply because it clears ordinary enemies impressively. A major encounter may leave different opportunities for applying the same action.

[Top Mortar](/database/modules/top-mortar/) has a projectile-count change, and [Fire Mage](/database/modules/fire-mage/) has a Firestorm damage change. Compare how each action fits your approach and the opening you want to use.

As a planning principle, distinguish spreading pressure across many threats from applying useful damage during a particular opening. Add the [boss preparation guide](/bosses/) to your reading when the next major fight, rather than ordinary survival, is the limiting factor.

</section>

<section className="step-card" id="step-7">

## Use artifacts only when their conditions are understood

An artifact should support something the setup actually does. Establish its trigger and affected action before assuming it strengthens every weapon or stacks multiplicatively with another effect.

Phoenix Feather is named in a fix involving Valkyrie, but that sentence alone is not a complete recipe. Electric Arrow's improved trigger reliability also does not disclose the entire trigger rule. The [artifact database](/database/artifacts/) keeps those limits visible.

If a public build depends on an interaction that you cannot confirm, evaluate the rest of the setup separately.

</section>

<section className="step-card" id="step-8">

## A practical comparison and a safe progression plan

Keep the test question small. Compare the same vehicle, captain, difficulty, and general conditions where possible, changing one relevant choice. Record what happened and what you could not control. Several successful attempts are more persuasive than a single unusually favorable run.

<div className="table-wrap">

| Build question | What to compare |
| --- | --- |
| Is offense more dependable? | Which action contributed during comparable openings |
| Is the setup easier to survive with? | What mistakes it recovered from and what still ended runs |
| Does a synergy work? | Both current descriptions and the observed trigger |
| Is it better for a boss? | The named encounter, difficulty, and repeat attempts |

</div>

For collection progress, an understandable and repeatable setup is a reasonable preference over a fragile experiment you cannot yet execute. Consult [progression](/guides/progression/) for unlock-based rerolls.

</section>
